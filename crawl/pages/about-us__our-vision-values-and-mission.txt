Vision, values and mission

Over the years we have evolved three phrases which express our vision, values and mission.

Our vision is to see the Church of Jesus Christ in Trowbridge (of which we are part) become :

‘A catalyst for God’s blessing to the Trowbridge area'

Our values might be summed up under the three headings:

‘Christ Focussed, Community Facing, Church Family’

Our mission is:

'To prepare the hearts of the people and the structures of the church for the next great move of God’
