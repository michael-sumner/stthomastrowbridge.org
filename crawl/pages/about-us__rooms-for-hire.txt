Rooms for hire

We have a variety of venues available for hire, of varying sizes, suitable for meetings, groups, and concerts.  These include our two churches, St Thomas' and Holy Trinity.  We have a hall and a suite of rooms adjacent to St Thomas'.  We welcome a wide range of community groups on a regular and one-off basis, from young people’s organisations to WI and U3A.

We love our rooms to be used, so if you need a space do get in touch and we will let you know our availability, or have a look on our bookings calendar below.

Please see our hire leaflet for more details, and if you have further questions, please don't hesitate to contact the Parish Office.

Room bookings/availability

This calendar is only an indication of room usage, and does not guranatee availability of rooms. Always contact our office to enquire about making a booking.
