Services

Our regular services are:

Sunday 10.30am Morning Service at St Thomas'  This is our main Sunday service when we enjoy lively worship, and children are able to meet in various junior church groups, except for the All Age Service on the 4th Sunday in the month when we all stay in church together.  All of these services are followed by refreshments.

1st Sunday - Holy Communion (Children go to their groups shortly after the start of the service and rejoin at about 11.30am)
2nd Sunday - Morning Service (Children in for the first 20 minutes then into their groups)
3rd Sunday - Morning Service
4th Sunday - All-age Service (Everyone stays in church together)
5th Sunday - Morning Service

"Encounter", first Sunday of each month at 7:00pm at St Thomas', a Spirit-filled, Spirit-led evening service. An hour with God, with space to encounter Him in prayer and worship

In addition, on the third Wednesday of the month, we have:

Wednesday 2.00pm at St Thomas', a spoken Book of Common Prayer Holy Communion, which is usally followed by refreshments.
