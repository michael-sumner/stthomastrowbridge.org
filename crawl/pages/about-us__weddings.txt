Weddings

We would love to talk to you about getting married in one of our churches.  Please contact the Parish Office and the Vicar will be happy to meet with you to discuss the possibility and what would be involved.  Please contact us in plenty of time, to allow for calling of Banns and marriage preparation.

If you live in St Thomas' parish, but are getting married in a different parish, you will need to have your Banns read in St Thomas' Church too.  Please contact the Parish office for information.

If you are not sure whether you live in St Thomas' parish or not, the parish map can be viewed here.
