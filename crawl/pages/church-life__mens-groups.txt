Men's groups

Men's Sports Group

Every Saturday morning the Sports Hall at Clarendon is booked at 9.00am for one hour, mainly to play volleyball.  Any man at church is welcome – no expertise is necessary!  They always manage to have fun and start the weekend with some exercise.  In the summer they had a few weeks playing outside.  There were also a few mornings when they had a go at walking football – which was lots more fun and more challenging than it sounds!

MenUnited - Trowbridge

St Thomas' has strong links with MenUnited - Trowbridge, an organisation which seeks to bring Christian men together and offer opportunities for outreach to their contacts.  Regular Breakfasts and Curry nights are held at the Rugby Club with visiting speakers, and are very well supported.  There is also a monthly lunch club at a local pub which offers an opportunity for men to get to know each other better.  Prayer is the basis of all these activities, and there are regular prayer meetings.

Tom's Shed

There's always something fun and exciting happening at Tom's Shed.  As well as activities it's also a great oppertunity to come and have some breathing space, and meet old and new friends over a cuppa. We like to keep our members informed about what's happening at the Shed and what's going on in the community. Shed outings are also a popular treat with at least four per year.

 

We are welcoming and inclusive, and we aim to create a connected community where men can drink tea, eat biscuits and have a chat.  There is also an opportunity for games and craft work etc.

 

Every Wednesday 09:30 to 12:30.  Join us at a time that suits you.

£2.50 per session including refreshments and some materials.

PARKING - is at the church, if full, there is local parking avalible in nearby streets.
