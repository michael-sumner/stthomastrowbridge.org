Pastoral care

Lay Pastoral Assistants are part of the Pastoral Team of the church consisting of the Vicar, Lay Licenced Ministers and Lay Pastoral Assistants (LPAs).

The team works alongside other groups and people to try and ensure that all members of the congregation are welcomed and cared for.  They are also responsible for the prayer section of the weekly newsletter and, when available, attend Baptism visits, coordinated by Liz Atkins, the pastoral coordinator.
