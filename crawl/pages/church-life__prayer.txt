Prayer

Prayer ministry is offered at the end of each service at St Thomas’ and Holy Trinity. This is available to anyone who would like someone to pray with them, and to be open to the Holy Spirit.

There is also prayer before the morning services at St Thomas', starting at 10:00. This is in the main church and anyone is welcome to join in.

We also hold a variety of midweek evening prayer meetings, usually about once a month. The pattern for these can vary, so have a look at our What's-on calendar page for details of these prayer events.
