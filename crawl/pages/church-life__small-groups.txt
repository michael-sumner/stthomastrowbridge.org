Small Groups in the life of St Thomas’s Church

Church services are really important in the life of any church; we can worship, learn and pray together as a whole church community. This is a vital part of our Christian discipleship.

However, getting together with other Christians in small groups for friendship and support in our daily lives, to learn from the Bible together and pray for each other, is also important for continuing as a Christian disciple.

For more detail about why small groups are important, click here.

For more detail about what happens in a Small Group, click here.

In St Thomas’s congregations, a variety of such Small Groups meet throughout the week on different days and at various times.

For more detail about when groups meet, click here.
