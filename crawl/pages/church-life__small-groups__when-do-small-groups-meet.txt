When and where do Small Groups meet?

Small Groups usually meet in homes; the venue may change from week to week. One group currently meets in the St Thomas’s Church Rooms. In general, Small Groups are most effective if the number of members doesn’t get too big, so not every group will have spare capacity.

 

If you would like to join a Small Group, please contact the Church Office.

The Small Group Coordinator will then get in touch to talk about what’s best for you.
