Why are Small Groups important?

There are three key purposes for Small Groups, reflecting the aims of the whole church.

UP To help members to grow personally in our Christian faith, deepening our relationship with God through worship, prayer and studying the Bible, becoming more like Jesus as a disciple.

IN To grow together as a group in love and care, supporting each other.

OUT To equip members to live as missional disciples, reaching out to others beyond the group through our personal life, work and witness, through social care, mission or evangelism.
