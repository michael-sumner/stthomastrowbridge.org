Worship

At St Thomas’ we have a heart to see God use our music to help bring us together to in worship to God, lead us into a place of openness for his Spirit to move and bring transformation in our lives so we become more like Jesus.

 

We use music in our Sunday services, prayer meetings and other events throughout the year, drawing on a team of musicians in the church and bringing a mix of contemporary and traditional styles for all ages. Alongside the musicians we have a music tech/sound team and audio-visual team who work together to provide excellent support for all our services and events.

 

If you have a heart for Jesus and are interested in joining any of these teams please contact the church office who will pass you on to the relevant team leader.
