Our churches

St Thomas' Parish in Trowbridge includes three churches: St Thomas', Holy Trinity (The Church on the Roundabout) and Kingfisher Church (meeting at the Mead School).

The Vicar is also responsible for the Parish of St John's, West Ashton.

This part of the website gives you more information on each of these churches.
