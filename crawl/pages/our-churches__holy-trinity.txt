Holy Trinity

Holy Trinity (The Church on the Roundabout) is a highly visible historic church (Grade II* listed) and a magnificent venue for all sorts of events.  Our vision for this building is to be a "house of prayer and worship", a resource for the whole church and community of Trowbridge, a catalyst for unity, and a venue for youth revival.  

We have a very busy Parent and Toddlers group which meets there on a Monday, The Island after school drop in on Tuesdays, and the popular Trowbridge Women’s Shed meets there every Wednesday morning. 

The stunning new stage at the front makes it an excellent performance space, as well as being a great venue for a wide range of events.  The kitchen was recently replaced to a high standard and is able to cater for a good number of guests.
