## Homepage, top to bottom

Mobile first. Large type. One idea per block.

1. **Hero.** Real photo of people. Heading: "Welcome to St Thomas' Trowbridge". Line: "Join us on Sundays at 10.30am." Buttons: **Plan your visit**, **See what's on**.
2. **This Sunday card.** Service type (Communion, Morning, All Age), time, short note for families. Fed by the single rule agreed for the All Age Sunday.
3. **Families strip.** Parent and Toddlers (Mondays in term time), Sunday groups for 0 to 18, Messy Church. One line: "Many of our families first met us at Parent and Toddlers." Button: **Families at St Thomas'**.
4. **Our family of churches.** Three cards: St Thomas', St John's, Holy Trinity. Name, one line, time, link.
5. **Faces and quotes.** Three to six consented photos and two short quotes. "Meet the team" link.
6. **For regulars.** Latest Weekly News (readable on page), Watch online, Calendar. Large buttons.
7. **Vision line.** "Christ Focussed, Community Facing, Church Family", plus a verse chosen by leadership (Andrew Palmer's proposal).
8. **Safeguarding line.** One sentence, link to policy and contacts.
9. **Find us.** Address, map, office hours, phone, email.

## Plan Your Visit page, outline

1. Sunday at 10.30am: what happens, how long (about an hour and a half), refreshments.
2. Children: groups by age, babies and toddlers space, DBS and safeguarding.
3. What to wear, what we ask of you (nothing), how we give.
4. Getting here: St Thomas Church, York Buildings, Trowbridge BA14 8PT. Parking. Step-free access `[to confirm]`.
5. Other services: first Sunday evening Encounter at 7pm (usually not November and December, check What's On), third Wednesday BCP Communion at 2pm.
6. Can't come? Watch online.
7. Print version.

## Writing rules for this audience

- British English, plain words, short sentences, no church jargon without a gloss ("Communion: sharing bread and wine").
- Invitational, not instructional: "You are welcome" beats "Please note".
- One phone number, one email, one contact form. No duplicate versions.
- No names of individuals unless they have agreed.
