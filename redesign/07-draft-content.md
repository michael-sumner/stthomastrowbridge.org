## Plan Your Visit: getting here

### Step-Free Access

St Thomas Church has step-free access. A ramp leads to the entrance, so wheelchairs, pushchairs and walking aids can come in without steps.

An accessible toilet, a hearing loop, seating for wheelchairs and large-print service sheets are available.

### Parking

There are a few parking spaces by the church. We keep these for older and disabled visitors. Several car parks are close by. Check the weekly news for any extra parking on the day.

### Find Us

St Thomas Church, York Buildings, Trowbridge BA14 8PT.
Church office: 01225 777331, office@stthomastrowbridge.org.

### Welcome

Parent and Toddler Group is for children from birth to school age and their parents or carers. Come along, meet other families and have a drink.

### When and Where

- **When:** Mondays during school term time, 10am to 11.45am.
- **Where:** Holy Trinity Church, Trowbridge, BA14 9AA.
- **Cost:** Free to attend. Donations are welcome.
- **Booking:** No booking needed. Just come along.

### What Happens

- Toys and a baby corner for the youngest.
- Crafts and singing.
- Refreshments for parents and children.

### Safe and Friendly

Volunteers who work with children have a current DBS check, and we follow the church safeguarding policy. The group is run by volunteers from St Thomas Church.

### Come and Meet Us

Call the church office on 01225 777331 or email office@stthomastrowbridge.org. You do not need to be a churchgoer to come.

### What Else Is on for Families

Sunday services at 10.30am have groups for children and young people. See Families for details.
