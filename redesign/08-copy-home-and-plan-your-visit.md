## Plan Your Visit

# Plan Your Visit

Everyone is welcome at St Thomas'. You will not be asked to do anything you do not want to do, and there is no dress code. Come as you are.

Print button: **Print this page**

## Sunday Services

**Every Sunday at 10.30am** at St Thomas Church, York Buildings, Trowbridge BA14 8PT. A service lasts about an hour and a half. Refreshments follow, so please stay for a drink and a chat.

| Sunday | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1st | Holy Communion. Children start in their groups and join the service at about 11.30am. |
| 2nd, 3rd and 5th | Morning Service. Children stay in for the first 20 minutes, then go to their groups. |
| 4th | All Age Service. Everyone stays together in church. |

The pattern can change in the school holidays and around Christmas, so check **What's On** for this week.

## What Happens at a Service

- We sing a mix of modern and traditional songs, led by our worship team.
- We pray and share church news.
- We hear the Bible read and explained in a talk of 15 to 30 minutes.
- Each month we share Communion.
- Afterwards, you can have prayer with our Prayer Ministry team if you wish, or chat over refreshments.

## Children and Young People

Children are welcome at every service and everyone starts together in church. Groups for each age run during the service:

- **Babies and toddlers:** a dedicated space at the back of church.
- **Scramblers and Climbers:** ages 3 to 7.
- **Explorers:** ages 7 to 11.
- **Pathfinders:** ages 11 to 14.
- **Apprentices:** ages 14 to 18.

Everyone who works with children has a current DBS check, and we follow our safeguarding policy. During the week, **Parent and Toddler Group** meets on Mondays in term time at Holy Trinity. See Families for more.

## Step-Free Access

St Thomas Church has step-free access. A ramp leads to the entrance, so wheelchairs, pushchairs and walking aids can come in without steps. An accessible toilet, a hearing loop, seating for wheelchairs and large-print service sheets are available.

## Parking

There are a few parking spaces by the church. We keep these for older and disabled visitors. Several car parks are close by. Check the Weekly News for any extra parking on the day.

## Giving

We do not take a collection during the service. If you wish to give, there is a plate at the entrance. You are never expected to.

## Other Services

- **Encounter:** first Sunday of the month at 7pm, an evening service of prayer and worship. It does not usually run in November and December, so check What's On.
- **Book of Common Prayer Holy Communion:** third Wednesday of the month at 2pm, followed by tea and cake. All are welcome.

## Watch Online

Most services are livestreamed. Watch on our [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@stthomaschurchtrowbridge) or find more worship on the Church of England website.

## Questions

Call the church office on 01225 777331 or email office@stthomastrowbridge.org. Office hours are Tuesday to Friday, 9.30am to 1pm.
