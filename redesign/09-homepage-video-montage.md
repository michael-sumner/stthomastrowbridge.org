# Homepage video montage: suggestion for the church secretary

Status: suggestion, 2026-10-20. Keep it small at first, add clips over time.

## Why

The old site shows buildings and stock photos. A short film of real people answers the question a newcomer has: what is it like, and are the people friendly? Parent and Toddler parents said the kindness drew them in. A montage shows that faster than text.

## What it is

- One montage of 8 to 12 clips, each 3 to 5 seconds, about 40 seconds in all.
- Silent, looping, with a visible Pause button.
- Sits in the hero area of the home page, not behind the text (see accessibility).
- Starts small and grows. New clips go in as the year turns.

## Shot list

| # | Clip | Notes | Season |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Worship band playing | Wide, then close on hands and instruments | All year |
| 2 | Hot drinks being poured and handed over | Hands and cups, no faces needed | All year |
| 3 | Drone: St Thomas Church and the surrounding area | Slow orbit or rise | Dry, calm days |
| 4 | Drone: Holy Trinity, "The Church on the Roundabout" | Distinctive landmark | Dry, calm days |
| 5 | Post-service chat over refreshments | Backs and side views unless people agree to be filmed | All year |
| 6 | Welcome at the door | A smile and a handshake | All year |
| 7 | Outdoor activities | Church weekend or family camping weekend, garden events, Messy Church if held outdoors | Summer |
| 8 | Parent and Toddler Group | Toys, crafts, hands. No children's faces without parental consent | Monday term time |

Start with clips 1, 2, 3 and 5 (about 15 seconds). Add the rest as they come in. Summer is the natural time for outdoor clips.

## Who, when and how

- **Filming:** Simon Wright (offered to take photos, to confirm he is happy to film). It needs a few visits: one Sunday, one Monday for Parent and Toddlers, one dry day for the drone.
- **Phone is fine:** landscape, 1080p, 30 frames per second, steady hands or a small gimbal. Hold each shot for 8 seconds so there is room to trim.
- **File naming:** `YYYY-MM-DD-subject.mp4`, for example `2026-11-01-worship-band.mp4`. Store in one shared folder, with a consent note per clip.
- **Editing:** one person stitches the clips (Paul Edwards or a volunteer). A free editor is enough. Export an MP4 and a poster image.

## Consent and safeguarding

- Anyone identifiable needs to agree. Parent consent for any child, in writing, collected by the church office (same process as photos).
- Prefer shots where faces do not matter: hands, backs, instruments, cups.
- Post a notice or announce on the Sunday of filming so people can step out of frame.
- Keep a list of clips and who agreed. Remove a clip promptly if anyone withdraws.

## Drone rules

- UK CAA rules apply. A camera drone of 100 grams or more normally needs the pilot to hold a Flyer ID and the operator to hold an Operator ID. Check the current rules on the CAA website before flying.
- No flying over crowds or people who are not involved. Film when the church grounds are empty, or with permission.
- Ask the PCC and any neighbours or landowners before filming. Holy Trinity sits on a roundabout, so check traffic and local rules.
- If nobody on the team holds the licences, use a local licensed pilot for a one-off shoot.

## Accessibility and performance (this audience is mostly 65 and over)

- Muted and looping, with a visible Pause and Play button, operable by keyboard. Do not autoplay with sound.
- Respect the device's reduced-motion setting: show the poster image and no motion.
- A text description beside the player, for example: "Short film of our worship band, hot drinks, the church from the air and people chatting after the service."
- No flashing or rapid cuts. Keep each clip 3 to 5 seconds with slow transitions.
- Keep the file small, ideally under 3MB, with a poster image so the page loads quickly on mobile data and older devices.
- Do not let the video push the Sunday time and Plan Your Visit button below the first screen.
- No music unless the church holds the licence. Silent is safest.
