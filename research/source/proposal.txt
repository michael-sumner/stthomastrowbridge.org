St Thomas’ Website Proposal – Andrew Palmer

In this report I have summarised some reflections on the current website
and also a basic proposal which streamlines the current information and
makes it more accessible, I hope. Using this report, I would ask that
you consider what you think and add to this so that we come up with a
firm proposal for me to work on should you wish. I am able to use the
current website builder and have assumed that we will continue with
that, should you want something else please let me know. I look forward
to hearing what you think.

Reflections on our current website:

- The design in impersonal, stock photos on the banner that do not
  represent the church and what we stand for

- Basic – good as basic means it is manageable but needs to be more
  organised and interesting

- Too wordy? If we keep things invitational and bite sized info that is
  clear that will be more inviting and easier to keep up to date.

- Need pictures of people or events not just buildings (we can do this
  with their consent)

- Too many tabs that repeat themselves, these can be streamlined.

Proposal:

Home page – Images of people and events. Names of the Churches, Times of
services, Vision and Values statement, a Bible Verse chosen by
leadership team, a sentence of invitation and welcome. Safeguarding
statement as required.

Tab 1: About us – staff/volunteers team list and photos, Vicars welcome
and photo (vision and values could go here?)

Tab 2: Churches and services info – times of services and regular
activities, address, picture and what to expect

Tab 3: Baptisms, Weddings and Funerals – Who to contact, info and
pictures

Tab 4: Online worship – You tube link on the page so each week people
can simply watch on the website if that is easier. A bit on info about
what worship is and how it works online. How to participate at home
guide. Sermon recordings could go here and any discipleship material you
wanted to share.

Tab 5: Notices and information for current members – could have sermon
notes, bible study group notes, notice sheets whatever is needed.
Calendar included here. One place everyone knows they can access for
useful information.

Tab 6: Rooms for hire info

Questions

- Do you want so much information about mission? Is this too much
  information for the website? Could you put this information into other
  things like a newsletter?

- Do you want a separate children and families tab?

- Are you happy gaining permission from people and using their pictures,
  who will do that part of the work?

- Do you want the CATA information on the website?

- Is there anything missing from the current website you want me to
  include, it helpful to be as specific as you can.

What I will need to build the website:

Access to an up to date computer with fast internet access. I can work
at home but do not have a computer with the right specs.

Information for each tab if that info is not already on the website or
known.

A bible verse for the home page

Agreed vision statement or values wording (If different from current
website)

Welcome statement from Allan and his picture

Worship at home guide wording

Photos that you have permission to use

Sermons or materials for current members – we will also need to think
about how this is managed because it will go out of date quickly.

Information from Allan and others involved on what they want to keep
from the current website and what they definitely want changing e.g. is
there any wording you particularly like I can simply cut and paste into
a new design?

Agreed dates to see the draft website so that I do not do too much
design work and then get to the end of the project and you are not happy
with it, this also means you get to help shape the website. (E.g. at
fortnightly intervals)

I estimate that the building of the website would take approx. 60 hours,
depending on others gathering some of the content e.g. pictures and
wording. I can’t be certain until I start but this is a good guide.
